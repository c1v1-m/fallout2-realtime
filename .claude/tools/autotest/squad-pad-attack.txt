# SQUAD COMMAND BY THE PAD: ATTACK to one companion (2026-11-12, his design "A"), with shots. A pistol raider 12 tiles
# in front of the dude is LT-locked (PE 10), so LT + A opens the mode with the cursor on it: red ATTACK over it, red
# corner brackets round it, white outlines round Vic and Sulik (who = All), the compass with Attack lit. The world
# slows for 1.5 s, then holds (shot 1). RB: who = Vic, the nearest (beside the dude; Sulik stands 5 tiles off) (shot 2).
# A: ATTACK for Vic alone -- his own focus, "On it." over him, the mode still up and the world still held (shot 3).
# B: the mode gone, the red chevron over the raider (shot 4); he shoots it. Sulik is not given it (he fights as he
# likes, on his own Distance).
# 2026-11-17: the mode stays open after an order (only B closes it): B tapped after the A, with a shot of the mode
# still up between them; the chevron and Vic's shot come once B lets the world run.
#   node ../fallout1-ce/.claude/tools/autotest/run.js .claude/tools/autotest/squad-pad-attack.txt 1 --exe build/Release/fallout2-ce.exe
name squad-pad-attack

dude tile 20100 rot 2

shot 2600
# RB: who = the nearest companion

shot 3400

expect \[INPUT/squad-a\] kind=enemy order=attack obj=\d+ tile=\d+ who=18062 ok=1
expect \[ACTION/squad-order\] order=attack target=16777454 companions=1 skipped=0 why=ok speaker=18062 t=[\d.]+ who=18062
expect \[FLOW/squad-focus\] own-on who=18062 id=\d+ pid=16777454
expect \[FLOW/squad-mark\] on id=\d+ pid=16777454
