# SQUAD COMMAND MODE: FIRST A SLOWDOWN, THEN THE STOP (2026-11-12, his pick: „ten efekt bude nejdříve zpomalení a pak
# zastavení. prostě prvně se bude zpomalovat 1-3 sekund třeba 1 nebo 2 a pak se zastaví“). The dude is hurt and Vic,
# 12 tiles off with a stimpak, is told HEAL ME; while he comes, LT + A opens the mode. The world eases from full speed
# to a standstill over 1.5 s of real time -- rt_clock and Vic (the probe: the critter on the move nearest the dude)
# advance less and less on every [FLOW/squad-mode] slow line -- then holds still exactly as under a radial wheel
# (world-hold on; neither rt_clock nor Vic moves until B). B: everything runs at full speed at once, Vic comes on and
# doses the dude. Then an order given DURING the slowdown (X, hold fire, 0.4 s after the open) is given at once and the
# world never holds. Then LT + D-pad (design B) opens nothing and slows nothing. (GO's walk to a post was the walker
# until GO went, 2026-11-13; GO came back on 2026-11-14, and the HEAL ME walk stayed the walker.)
# 2026-11-17: the mode stays open after an order (only B closes it): B tapped after the X, 0.4 s later and still in
# the slowdown, so that open never reaches the hold (one freeze and one world-hold in all, as before), and design B's
# LT + D-pad comes with the mode closed (an open mode would take the D-pad as its own FOLLOW).
#   node ../fallout1-ce/.claude/tools/autotest/run.js .claude/tools/autotest/squad-mode-slow.txt 1 --exe build/Release/fallout2-ce.exe
name squad-mode-slow
map DESERT1.MAP
seed 1
duration 13
dude tile 20100 rot 2
dude hp 400
dude curhp 200
dude armor 2

spawn 16777278 20112 rot 5 wield 8 item 29 3 item 40 1 party

# Vic comes to dose him (HEAL ME, 12 tiles: rt_step_toward's 4-tile legs, run while his AP lasts)
squad 300 healme 16777278
# ...and 0.7 s into his walk, LT + A: the mode opens, the world slows for 1.5 s, then holds
axis 800 LT 32767
tap 1000 A
axis 1300 LT -32768
# held for a while; B: the world runs again, at once
tap 4500 B
# an order in the slowdown: LT + A, X 0.4 s later -- HOLD FIRE given at once; the mode stays for the next order, and
# B 0.4 s after the X (0.8 s after the open, still in the slowdown) closes it before the hold
axis 7000 LT 32767
tap 7200 A
axis 7400 LT -32768
tap 7600 X
tap 8000 B
# design B: LT + D-pad Down (FOLLOW) -- no mode, no slowdown
axis 9500 LT 32767
tap 9800 DOWN
axis 10100 LT -32768
note 12500 end

# the checks (run.js)
# 1. the open, with Vic on the move as the probe
expect \[FLOW/squad-mode\] open why=lt\+a slow=1\.50 probe=\d+ probePid=16777278
# the slowdown: lines from nearly full speed down to the floor, rt_clock creeping less and less
expect \[FLOW/squad-mode\] slow f=0\.9\d after=0\.\d\d rt=\+0\.(0[5-9]|1[0-3])\d probe=\d+ moved=\d+
expect \[FLOW/squad-mode\] slow f=0\.[3-6]\d after=0\.\d\d rt=\+0\.0[1-7]\d probe=\d+ moved=\d+
expect \[FLOW/squad-mode\] slow f=0\.0\d after=1\.\d\d rt=\+0\.0[01]\d probe=\d+ moved=[0-2] dude=
expect-count >=10 \[FLOW/squad-mode\] slow f=
# ...and then the hold, after 1.5 s of real time and about half of that on rt_clock -- this open's only: section 2's
# mode is closed by B in its slowdown, so one freeze in all
expect \[FLOW/squad-mode\] freeze after=1\.5\d rtSlowed=0\.[5-9]\d\d probe=\d+
expect-count 1 \[FLOW/squad-mode\] freeze
expect \[FLOW/world-hold\] on why=squad
# B: nothing moved while it held -- rt_clock to the millisecond, Vic to the pixel
expect \[FLOW/squad-mode\] close why=b slowed=1\.5\d frozen=1 heldMs=\d+ rtHeld=0\.000 probeHeld=0
expect \[FLOW/world-hold\] off
# ...and Vic came on and dosed him afterwards
expect \[FLOW/squad-heal\] dose id=\d+ pid=16777278 item=40 kind=1 ok=1
# 2. an order in the slowdown, given at once: X is the mode's (its stays line), and that mode was up only in its
# slowdown -- B closed it at 0.8 s, frozen=0, and the one freeze above is section 1's -- so the order came before any
# hold, and the world never held for it
expect \[ACTION/squad-order\] order=hold-fire target=- companions=1 skipped=0 why=ok speaker=\d+ t=[\d.]+ who=all
expect-count 1 \[INPUT/squad-mode\] stays after=x order=hold-fire
expect \[FLOW/squad-mode\] close why=b slowed=0\.[6-9]\d frozen=0 heldMs=0
expect-count 1 \[FLOW/world-hold\] on why=squad
# both modes were closed by B, nothing else
expect-count 2 \[FLOW/squad-mode\] close why=
expect-count 2 \[FLOW/squad-mode\] close why=b slowed=
# 3. design B's LT + D-pad opens no mode (and the mode's own D-pad took nothing: it was closed by then)
expect \[ACTION/squad-order\] order=follow target=- companions=1 skipped=0 why=ok speaker=\d+ t=[\d.]+ who=all
expect-count 2 \[FLOW/squad-mode\] open why=
expect-not \[INPUT/squad-dpad\]
