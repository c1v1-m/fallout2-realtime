# SQUAD COMMAND (2026-11-13, his pick when GO went: the LT + A mode stays without GO, and Y in it switches their healing
# of him -- „Y No healing“): NO HEALING, then HEAL AGAIN, given straight to combat_rt -- the medic opt-out the party
# dialogue line toggles („(Stop patching me up. Save your supplies.)“), for All.
# The dude is at 150 of 400 HP, under the medic's 50 %. Vic (10mm pistol, five stimpaks) stands beside him: HOLD keeps
# him there (the medic doses within 3 tiles) and HOLD FIRE keeps the fight on -- he never shoots the knife raider that
# comes at the dude from off the screen at 1 s, so nobody ends it, and one holding his fire still heals (the medic is a
# fighter's beat). NO HEALING before it all: his medic beat never doses the dude (rt_medic_patient passes him over) --
# yet HEAL ME at 5 s is answered, it goes past the opt-out: he asked. HEAL AGAIN at 10 s: the beat doses the dude
# again, once the 6 s cooldown of the HEAL ME dose has run out. NO HEALING / HEAL AGAIN write no Distance (HOLD's Stay
# stays).
#   node ../fallout1-ce/.claude/tools/autotest/run.js .claude/tools/autotest/squad-noheal.txt 1 --exe build/Release/fallout2-ce.exe
name squad-noheal
map DESERT1.MAP
seed 1
duration 20
dude tile 20100 rot 2
dude hp 400
dude curhp 150
dude armor 2

spawn 16777278 20102 rot 2 wield 8 item 29 3 item 40 5 party
spawn 16777455 16700 rot 2 wield 4 team 50

# Vic stays beside him and holds his fire; no healing of the dude from him
squad 100 hold all
squad 150 holdfire all
squad 200 noheal all
# the fight: the knife raider turns on the dude
hostile 1000 16777455
# A on the dude (HEAL ME): past the opt-out
squad 5000 healme all
# HEAL AGAIN (what Y gives once every one of who keeps his supplies)
squad 10000 healagain all
note 19000 end

# the checks (run.js)
expect \[FLOW/autotest\] squad t=\d+ order=noheal who=-1 whoId=-1 arg=-1 ok=1
expect \[ACTION/squad-order\] order=no-healing target=- companions=1 skipped=0 why=ok speaker=18062 t=[\d.]+ who=all
expect \[FLOW/medic-optout\] id=18062 treats=0 count=1
expect \[FLOW/autotest\] hostile t=\d+ pid=16777455 id=\d+
# the fight was on for him: he would take the raider, but holds his fire
expect \[FLOW/squad-fire\] refuse id=18062 pid=16777278 tgt=\d+ tgtPid=16777455
# HEAL ME goes past the opt-out
expect \[FLOW/squad-heal\] dose id=18062 pid=16777278 item=40 kind=1 ok=1
expect \[FLOW/medic\] id=18062 pid=16777278 patient=18000 self=0 kind=1 item=40 .*why=heal-me
expect \[FLOW/autotest\] squad t=\d+ order=healagain who=-1 whoId=-1 arg=-1 ok=1
expect \[ACTION/squad-order\] order=heal-again target=- companions=1 skipped=0 why=ok speaker=18062 t=[\d.]+ who=all
expect \[FLOW/medic-optout\] id=18062 treats=1 count=0
# ...and his medic beat doses the dude again
expect \[FLOW/medic\] id=18062 pid=16777278 patient=18000 self=0 kind=1 item=40 .* ap=\d+ t=1\d\.\d+ why=beat
# held: no dose of the beat's to him before HEAL AGAIN (t is rt_clock, about the timeline's seconds); his Distance
# untouched by either order (a Distance table entry left out would write Stay close)
expect-not \[FLOW/medic\] id=18062 pid=16777278 patient=18000 .* ap=\d+ t=\d\.\d+ why=beat
expect-not \[FLOW/party-distance\] id=\d+ pid=16777278 dist=stay-close was=stay
