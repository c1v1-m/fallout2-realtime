# SQUAD COMMAND (2026-11-12, his design "A"): a post in a fight is Distance "Stay" at that tile.
# Vic is sent to 20108 (4 tiles left of where he stands, 8 left of the dude) as a pistol raider 10 tiles right of the
# dude comes into the fight. At his post Vic takes no step for the fight -- no chase leg, no tactic's step, no follow --
# and shoots the raider from there (14 tiles: in a 10mm pistol's reach), and the post holds the whole fight.
#   node ../fallout1-ce/.claude/tools/autotest/run.js .claude/tools/autotest/squad-go-fight.txt 1 --exe build/Release/fallout2-ce.exe
name squad-go-fight
map DESERT1.MAP
seed 1
duration 20
dude tile 20100 rot 2
dude stat PE 8
dude hp 400
dude armor 2

spawn 16777278 19904 rot 1 wield 8 item 29 3 party
spawn 16777454 22100 rot 5 wield 8 item 29 2 hostile hp 30

squad 300 go 16777278 20108
note 19000 end

# the checks (run.js)
expect \[FLOW/squad-post\] set id=\d+ pid=16777278 tile=20108
expect \[FLOW/squad-post\] arrive id=\d+ pid=16777278 tile=20108
expect \[FLOW/party-distance\] id=\d+ pid=16777278 dist=stay
expect \[COMBAT/attack\] NPC aid=\d+ ->pid=16777454
# Stay at the post: none of the fight's movers, and the post never left
expect-not \[FLOW/(walk-seg|chase-move|back-off|spread-step|party-leash|follow-step|flank-start|firepos)\] id=\d+ pid=16777278
expect-not \[FLOW/squad-post\] (off|end) id=\d+ pid=16777278
