# SCREEN: ATTACK ON A NEUTRAL IN THE SQUAD COMMAND MODE (2026-11-14, his „Ano, i na neutrály“). In Gecko's junkyard
# the cursor on a peaceful ghoul left the compass's "Attack" grey and A buzzed; now a neutral takes ATTACK as an enemy
# does, in the yellow the LT lock gives a neutral (red stays an enemy's). Vic (10mm pistol) 4 tiles left of the dude,
# Sulik (knife) right below him; a ghoul (the pid of his Gecko ghoul), neutral, 8 tiles in front. LT locks it (nobody
# fights him: the neutral in front), A opens the mode with the cursor on it.
# 2026-11-17: the mode stays open after an order (only B closes it): B tapped after the ATTACK (half a second later),
# so the world runs again -- the chevron goes up over the ghoul and their blow starts the fight. Shots (853x480):
#   1 after the hold: ATTACK in YELLOW over the ghoul, yellow corner brackets round it, white outlines round Vic and
#     Sulik (who = All), the compass's "Attack" white, the legend
#     "LB RB All  A Attack  RS Next companion  X Hold fire  Y No healing  LS Keep distance  B Back"
#   2 A: ATTACK to All -- given, a click, and the mode is still up: "On it." over the nearest, where his name was
#   3 B: the mode closed; the red chevron over the ghoul (the focus)
#   4 the fight their order started: the ghoul turned on them
#   node ../fallout1-ce/.claude/tools/autotest/run.js .claude/tools/autotest/screen-squad-neutral.txt 1 --exe build/Release/fallout2-ce.exe
name screen-squad-neutral
map DESERT1.MAP
seed 1
duration 10
dude tile 20100 rot 2
dude stat PE 10
dude hp 400
dude armor 2

spawn 16777278 19904 rot 5 wield 8 item 29 3 party
spawn 16777313 20302 rot 4 wield 4 party
spawn 16777375 21700 rot 5

# LT down (locks the ghoul), A = the mode with the cursor on it; LT up -- the mode stays; the world holds from ~2.3 s
axis 600 LT 32767
tap 800 A
axis 1100 LT -32768
shot 2600
# A: ATTACK to All -- it clicks and the mode stays (the world still held)
tap 3000 A
shot 3300
# B: the mode closes and the world runs -- the chevron, then their blow
tap 3500 B
shot 3900
shot 6500
note 9000 end

# the checks (run.js)
expect \[FLOW/autotest-spawn\] id=\d+ pid=16777375 tile=21700 wield=-1 hostile=0 team=[1-9]\d*
expect \[ACTION/lock-on\] target=\d+ src=LT
expect \[INPUT/squad-mode\] open members=2 start=lock x=\d+ y=\d+ tile=\d+ kind=neutral
expect \[INPUT/squad-cursor\] on kind=neutral ok=1 obj=\d+ pid=16777375
expect \[INPUT/squad-a\] kind=neutral order=attack obj=\d+ tile=\d+ who=all ok=1
expect \[ACTION/squad-order\] order=attack target=16777375 companions=2 skipped=0 why=ok speaker=\d+ t=[\d.]+ who=all
expect \[FLOW/squad-focus\] on id=\d+ pid=16777375 tile=\d+ hostile=0
# the order clicks and the mode stays; B alone closes it, after the hold -- which held all along (rtHeld 0)
expect-count 1 \[INPUT/squad-mode\] stays after=a order=attack speaker=\d+
expect-count 1 \[INPUT/squad-mode\] close why=
expect \[INPUT/squad-mode\] close why=b
expect \[FLOW/squad-mode\] close why=b slowed=1\.5\d frozen=1 heldMs=\d+ rtHeld=0\.000
# the world runs: the chevron over the focus; the order made it nobody's enemy, their blow did
expect \[FLOW/squad-mark\] on id=\d+ pid=16777375
expect \[FLOW/newhostile\] pid=16777375 whoDude=0 whoParty=1
# the A was the mode's both times: no interaction, no attack of the dude's own; no refusal of a neutral
expect-not \[ACTION/interact-(queue|do)\]
expect-not \[COMBAT/attack\] DUDE
expect-not \[ACTION/squad-order\] .*why=neutral
expect-not \[INPUT/squad-a\] kind=neutral .*ok=0
