# SQUAD COMMAND (2026-11-12, his design "A"): GO to All -- one post each round the tile -- then FOLLOW ends the posts.
# Vic (4 tiles from 20108) and Sulik (8 tiles from it). GO 20108 for All: the nearest to it, Vic, gets the tile itself,
# Sulik the nearest free tile round it (RT_SQUAD_GO_SPREAD, ring 1 of 20108). Both walk there and arrive. At 7 s FOLLOW
# for All -- the regroup -- ends both posts (why=follow), and their Distance is back (Stay close).
#   node ../fallout1-ce/.claude/tools/autotest/run.js .claude/tools/autotest/squad-go-all.txt 1 --exe build/Release/fallout2-ce.exe
