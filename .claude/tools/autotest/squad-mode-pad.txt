# SQUAD COMMAND MODE by the pad: its buttons (2026-11-12, his design "A"). LT + A opens the mode (the world slows,
# then holds), B closes it with no order; LT + A then X gives HOLD FIRE to All and closes it; LT + A, RB (Sulik, the
# nearest), RB (Vic), D-pad Down gives FOLLOW to Vic alone and closes it; LT + A then Y (his pick of 2026-11-13) gives
# NO HEALING to All and closes it. The opening A is the mode's: no interaction, no loot, no use-on cursor. The cursor's
# orders by the pad are squad-pad-go, -go-fight, -heal, -weapon, -attack, -holdfire, -noheal (A on free ground is GO
# again since 2026-11-14: squad-pad-go); the slowdown and the hold are squad-mode-slow; the pictures are
# screen-squad-mode and squad-pad-attack.
# 2026-11-17: the mode stays open after an order (only B closes it): B tapped after the X, the D-pad's FOLLOW and the
# Y; each order keeps its own opening (B, then LT + A again), so each starts from a fresh mode (who = All) and every
# opening A is still checked to be no order -- an A in a mode left open would be GO on the ground under the cursor.
# Vic (pistol) 4 tiles left of the dude; Sulik (knife) beside him; no enemy, so nothing moves the world but the orders.
#   node ../fallout1-ce/.claude/tools/autotest/run.js .claude/tools/autotest/squad-mode-pad.txt 1 --exe build/Release/fallout2-ce.exe
name squad-mode-pad
map DESERT1.MAP
seed 1
duration 14
dude tile 20100 rot 2
dude hp 400
dude curhp 200
dude armor 2

spawn 16777278 19904 rot 5 wield 8 item 29 3 item 40 2 party
spawn 16777313 20300 rot 4 wield 4 party

# 1. LT + A: the mode opens; B (in the slowdown): it closes with no order.
axis 500 LT 32767
tap 700 A
tap 1500 B
axis 1800 LT -32768
# 2. LT + A, X (after the hold): HOLD FIRE to All; the mode stays; B closes it (the world still held).
axis 2500 LT 32767
tap 2700 A
tap 4600 X
tap 4900 B
axis 5200 LT -32768
# 3. LT + A, RB (who = the nearest companion, Sulik), RB (Vic), D-pad Down: FOLLOW to Vic alone; the mode stays;
#    B closes it (1.4 s after the open: still in the slowdown).
axis 5500 LT 32767
tap 5700 A
tap 6100 RB
tap 6400 RB
tap 6800 DOWN
tap 7100 B
axis 7400 LT -32768
# 4. LT + A, Y (after the hold): NO HEALING to All; the mode stays; B closes it (the world still held).
axis 8000 LT 32767
tap 8200 A
tap 10100 Y
tap 10400 B
axis 10700 LT -32768

# the checks (run.js)
expect-count 4 \[FLOW/squad-mode\] open why=lt\+a slow=1\.50
# every close is B's: four in all, one with no order (in the slowdown), three after an order that left the mode open
expect-count 4 \[FLOW/squad-mode\] close why=
expect-count 4 \[FLOW/squad-mode\] close why=b slowed=
expect-count 3 \[INPUT/squad-mode\] stays after=
expect \[FLOW/squad-mode\] close why=b slowed=0\.\d\d frozen=0
expect \[INPUT/squad-mode\] stays after=x order=hold-fire speaker=\d+
expect \[ACTION/squad-order\] order=hold-fire target=- companions=2 skipped=0 why=ok speaker=\d+ t=[\d.]+ who=all
expect \[INPUT/squad-who\] who=\w+ id=18097 pid=16777313 index=0 of=2
expect \[INPUT/squad-who\] who=\w+ id=18062 pid=16777278 index=1 of=2
expect \[INPUT/squad-dpad\] dir=down order=follow who=18062 ok=1
expect \[ACTION/squad-order\] order=follow target=- companions=1 skipped=0 why=ok speaker=18062 t=[\d.]+ who=18062
expect \[INPUT/squad-mode\] stays after=dpad order=follow speaker=18062
expect \[INPUT/squad-y\] order=no-healing who=all ok=1
expect \[ACTION/squad-order\] order=no-healing target=- companions=2 skipped=0 why=ok speaker=\d+ t=[\d.]+ who=all
expect \[FLOW/medic-optout\] id=18062 treats=0
expect \[FLOW/medic-optout\] id=18097 treats=0
expect \[INPUT/squad-mode\] stays after=y order=no-healing speaker=\d+
# X and Y came after the hold, and the world held on through the order until B 0.3 s later (rt_clock did not move):
# two such closes, each held well over 0.1 s (section 3's B comes 1.4 s after its open, still in its slowdown)
expect-count 2 \[FLOW/squad-mode\] close why=b slowed=1\.5\d frozen=1 heldMs=\d{3,} rtHeld=0\.000
# the mode opens on free ground (GO's, since 2026-11-14), but no A there: no post was set
expect \[INPUT/squad-mode\] open members=2 start=ahead x=\d+ y=\d+ tile=\d+ kind=ground
expect-not \[FLOW/squad-post\] set
# the opening A is spent: no interaction (interact_prompt's request / its walk-then-act), no world click
expect-not \[ACTION/interact-(queue|do)\]
expect-not \[INPUT/key\] code=-2
