# SCREEN TEST (Fallout 2 Realtime 2026-11-15, his „dáme tlačítka k pokládání bomby, ať nemusí hráč left joystickem
# vybírat příkazy“, his pick „Varianta 1“): the bomb's SET TIMER window names its pad buttons. Opened from the RB quick
# slot (plastic explosive bound to it), as he opens it. The first shot is the window at rest: A on DONE, B on CANCEL,
# X on PLACE, the D-pad (up and down lit) right of the clock, the pointer hidden. The left stick snaps the pointer onto
# a button -- the arrow comes back (second shot) -- and 2 s of rest hide it again (third shot). Then the D-pad takes
# the clock down to 0:10 and X places it: the window's own buttons, unchanged.
name screen-timer-marks
map DESERT1.MAP
seed 1
duration 16
dude tile 20100 rot 2
dude hp 300
dude skill traps 10
dude item 85 2
dude quickslot rb 85

tap 1500 RB
shot 2600
# the left stick: one snap onto a button
axis 3000 LX 32767
axis +600 LX 0
shot +400
note +2600 two seconds of rest
shot +100
tap +400 DOWN
tap +400 DOWN
tap +400 DOWN
tap +400 DOWN
tap +400 DOWN
shot +500
tap +500 X
shot +1200
