# TESTER (2026-11-15, area b): Y and BACK HELD through the close of the bomb's SET TIMER window, opened from the WORLD
# (the RB quick slot). Y = 3:00 in the window; A = DONE while Y and BACK are both still down; both held 0.9 s into the
# world. A Y hold is the weapon wheel (a wheel holds the world: world-hold on) and its release the hand swap; a BACK
# release is the inventory, a BACK hold the run to the nearest exit. None of them may come of presses made in the
# window. Then the right stick in a second window: the arrow comes back (shown), 2 s of rest hide it, and A is DONE.
name test-timer-world-y-back-held
map DESERT1.MAP
seed 1
duration 20
dude tile 20100 rot 2
dude hp 300
dude skill traps 150
dude item 85 2
dude quickslot rb 85

tap 1500 RB
shot 2600
# Y (3:00) and BACK down, A = DONE while both are held
pad +500 Y 1
pad +300 BACK 1
tap +300 A
shot +400
# both let go 0.9 s after the close
pad +500 Y 0
pad +0 BACK 0
shot +800
# the second window: the right stick moves the pointer (the arrow shows), 2.5 s of rest (hidden), A = DONE
tap +1500 RB
axis +1500 RX 20000
axis +250 RX 0
shot +200
note +2600 rest
shot +100
tap +300 A
shot +1500
note +500 done
