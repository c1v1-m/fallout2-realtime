# SQUAD ORDERS (2026-11-11, his design "B"): LT + D-pad Up = ATTACK, the LT-locked enemy is the squad's focus.
# Vic (10mm pistol, his own Distance Stay close) faces a knife raider 9 tiles off (behind the dude, nearer Vic than
# anything else) and picks it on his own. The dude faces a pistol raider 12 tiles off in front (15 from Vic); LT locks
# that one (in front beats behind, PE 10 = reach 20) and Up makes it the focus. Vic must turn on the focus at once
# (squad-focus take) -- although it is not the nearest to him, he does not even see it, and it stands past his Stay
# close leash (ATTACK outranks FOLLOW's leash) -- and shoot it; the red chevron sits over it (squad-mark).
# At 9 s LT + Down = FOLLOW, the regroup: it ends the focus, and the chevron goes with it.
# The raider has 30 HP so it lives to the FOLLOW: Vic hits 10-20 % at that range (a kill would end the focus first).
#   node ../fallout1-ce/.claude/tools/autotest/run.js .claude/tools/autotest/squad-attack.txt 1 --exe build/Release/fallout2-ce.exe
name squad-attack
map DESERT1.MAP
seed 1
duration 16
dude tile 20100 rot 2
dude stat PE 10
dude hp 400
dude armor 2

spawn 16777278 19904 rot 5 wield 8 item 29 3 party
spawn 16777455 18500 rot 2 wield 4 hostile hp 30
spawn 16777454 22500 rot 5 wield 8 item 29 2 hostile hp 30

# LT down (locks the pistol raider in front), Up = ATTACK, LT up (the lock goes; the focus stays)
axis 600 LT 32767
tap 800 UP
axis 1200 LT -32768
# LT down, Down = FOLLOW (the regroup), LT up
axis 9000 LT 32767
tap 9200 DOWN
axis 9600 LT -32768
note 14000 end

# the checks (run.js)
expect \[ACTION/lock-on\] target=\d+ src=LT
expect \[ACTION/squad-order\] order=attack target=16777454 companions=1 skipped=0 why=ok speaker=\d+
expect \[FLOW/squad-focus\] on id=\d+ pid=16777454
expect \[FLOW/squad-mark\] on id=\d+ pid=16777454
expect \[FLOW/squad-focus\] take id=\d+ pid=16777278 tgt=\d+ tgtPid=16777454
expect \[COMBAT/attack\] NPC aid=\d+ ->pid=16777454
expect \[ACTION/squad-order\] order=follow target=- companions=1 skipped=0 why=ok
expect \[FLOW/squad-focus\] off id=\d+ pid=16777454 why=follow
expect \[FLOW/squad-mark\] off
# the lock let go of with LT is not the end of the focus, nor is anything else before the FOLLOW
expect-not \[FLOW/squad-focus\] off .*why=(replaced|gone|map|dead)
