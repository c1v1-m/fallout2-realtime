# SQUAD COMMAND MODE: D-PAD UP ON A NEUTRAL (2026-11-14, his „Ano, i na neutrály“). Inside the mode the D-pad is
# design B's four for who, and Up is ATTACK on what the cursor is on -- a neutral as an enemy, the same test as A's
# (gpui_squad_on_attackable). Vic (10mm pistol) 4 tiles left of the dude, Sulik (knife) right below him; a ghoul (the pid
# of his Gecko ghoul), neutral, 8 tiles in front. LT locks it (nobody fights him: the neutral in front), A opens the
# mode with the cursor on it, LT up; D-pad Up gives ATTACK to All and closes the mode, and their blow turns the ghoul.
# A second opening is no test of it: after the order the ghoul is hostile, an enemy.
#   node ../fallout1-ce/.claude/tools/autotest/run.js .claude/tools/autotest/squad-pad-neutral-up.txt 1 --exe build/Release/fallout2-ce.exe
name squad-pad-neutral-up
map DESERT1.MAP
seed 1
duration 8
dude tile 20100 rot 2
dude stat PE 10
dude hp 400
dude armor 2

spawn 16777278 19904 rot 5 wield 8 item 29 3 party
spawn 16777313 20302 rot 4 wield 4 party
spawn 16777375 21700 rot 5

# LT down (locks the ghoul), A = the mode with the cursor on it; LT up -- the mode stays; the world holds from ~2.3 s
axis 600 LT 32767
tap 800 A
axis 1100 LT -32768
# D-pad Up: ATTACK to All on the neutral under the cursor, and the mode closes
tap 3000 UP
note 7000 end

# the checks (run.js)
expect \[FLOW/autotest-spawn\] id=\d+ pid=16777375 tile=21700 wield=-1 hostile=0 team=[1-9]\d*
expect \[INPUT/squad-mode\] open members=2 start=lock x=\d+ y=\d+ tile=\d+ kind=neutral
expect \[INPUT/squad-dpad\] dir=up order=attack who=all ok=1
expect \[ACTION/squad-order\] order=attack target=16777375 companions=2 skipped=0 why=ok speaker=\d+ t=[\d.]+ who=all
expect \[FLOW/squad-focus\] on id=\d+ pid=16777375 tile=\d+ hostile=0
expect \[INPUT/squad-mode\] close why=dpad
expect \[FLOW/squad-mode\] close why=dpad slowed=1\.5\d frozen=1
# the order made it nobody's enemy; their blow did
expect \[FLOW/newhostile\] pid=16777375 whoDude=0 whoParty=1
# Up was the mode's: never refused on the neutral, no Pip-Boy, no attack of the dude's own
expect-not \[INPUT/squad-dpad\] dir=up .*ok=0
expect-not \[ACTION/squad-order\] .*why=neutral
expect-not \[COMBAT/attack\] DUDE
